# ADA / Texas Accessibility Standards signage — H2SEP

_Home2 Suites by Hilton · 115 keys · Eagle Pass, Maverick County, Texas. Researched
2026-08-20 against the 2010 ADA Standards (Access Board) and the 2012 Texas Accessibility
Standards (TDLR primary documents). Companion files: `CBC-vs-TAS-DELTA.md` (what G401 gets
wrong), `PROJECT-FINDINGS.md` (what the permit set says), `sign-schedule.md` (counts)._

## Six premises we started with that are wrong

These change what gets bought, so they lead. Each was checked against primary text.

| # | Common belief | What the standards actually say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "70% contrast is required" | **False.** Neither 2010 ADA nor 2012 TAS specifies any contrast percentage. §703.5.1 requires only a **non-glare finish** and **light-on-dark or dark-on-light**. The Access Board: *"A minimum level of color contrast is not specified in the Standards."* The 70% figure is a legacy **1991 ADAAG appendix** recommendation — advisory, never enforceable. |
| 2 | "Texas requires geometric restroom symbols (triangle/circle)" | **False — that is California** (CBC 11B-703.7.2.6). Zero occurrences of "geometric", "triangle" or "equilateral" in 2012 TAS **or** 1994 TAS. Independently verified twice. **Do not buy triangle/circle restroom signs.** |
| 3 | "Tex. Transp. Code §681.009/§681.011 dictates the parking sign wording" | **Wrong instrument.** §681.011 sets *fines*; §681.009 *delegates* to the TDLR standards. The actual mandate is **16 TAC §68.104** — see below. |
| 4 | "There's a transient-lodging exception in §216.2" | **No such exception exists.** §216.2 has exactly one exception, for exterior signs *not* at the door they serve. **Every one of the 115 guest room doors needs a tactile + Grade 2 Braille sign.** No hotel carve-out. |
| 5 | "§216.8 = TTY, §216.9 = ALS, §216.11 = stairs, §216.12 = elevators" | **Scrambled.** Correct: 216.6 Entrances · 216.7 Elevators · 216.8 Toilet/Bathing · 216.9 TTYs · 216.10 ALS · 216.11 Check-Out Aisles · 216.12 **Amusement Rides**. |
| 6 | "ADA has stairway sign requirements" | **It has none.** §504/§505 contain no signage provisions. **All stair signage on this job is IBC-driven** — coordinate with the fire marshal, not the RAS. |

## The Texas enforcement layer

TAS compliance is a **state licensing matter with a physical inspector**, on top of federal
ADA civil-rights exposure.

| Item | Requirement | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Governing law | Texas Architectural Barriers Act, **Tex. Gov't Code Ch. 469**, administered by TDLR | Gov't Code Ch. 469 |
| Standard | **2012 TAS**, effective 2012-03-15, based on the 2010 ADA Standards | 2012 TAS |
| Applies to hotels? | **Yes, expressly** — *"an inn, hotel, motel, or other place of lodging"* (>5 rooms) | 16 TAC §68.20(d)(1) |
| Registration threshold | **$50,000** estimated construction cost → mandatory TDLR registration + RAS plan review | Gov't Code §469.101; 16 TAC §68.50(a) |
| Submission deadline | Design professional submits **≤20 days** after plans/specs are issued | 16 TAC §68.50(b) |
| RAS inspection | Owner must obtain inspection **≤1 year after completion**; Request for Inspection filed **≤30 days** after completion | 16 TAC §68.52(a) |
| Fees | **$175** filing · **$300** late · **$215/hr** special review | 16 TAC §68.80 |
| Technical Memoranda | 24 active TMs. **None address signage.** | 16 TAC §68.100 |

A 115-key hotel vastly exceeds $50k. **Registration, RAS plan review and RAS inspection are
all mandatory, and the owner — not the architect, not the GC — is legally responsible**
(§469.101, §469.105).

## Texas accessible parking — 16 TAC §68.104

**This is the single largest Texas-specific add, and it is not in the TAS document.** It is a
separate TDLR rule, effective **August 1, 2020**, implementing **HB 3163 (86th Leg., 2019)** —
which is why a search of the TAS PDF alone misses it. Verified verbatim.

**§68.104(a) — every paved accessible parking space must include:**

1. The **International Symbol of Accessibility painted conspicuously on the surface** in a
   color that contrasts the pavement.
2. The words **"NO PARKING"** painted on any access aisle adjacent to the space — **all
   capital letters, letter height ≥12 inches, stroke width ≥2 inches, centered within each
   adjacent access aisle**.
3. A consequences sign stating at minimum **"Violators Subject to Fine and Towing"** in
   **letter height ≥1 inch**; mounted on a pole, post, wall or freestanding board; **no more
   than 8 inches below** the TAS 502.6 sign; **bottom edge no lower than 48" and no higher
   than 80" above ground level**.

**§68.104(b):** a TAS 502.6 identification sign that already includes the (a)(3)(A) wording
satisfies (a)(3) — i.e. one combined sign is acceptable.

> **No dollar amount is required on the sign.** For owner awareness only, Tex. Transp. Code
> §681.011 fines run **$500–$750** first offense up to **$1,250 + 50 hrs** community service.
> ⚠ **§681.011(f-2): a peace officer may issue only a warning, not a citation, if the space
> lacks a compliant identification sign.** A non-compliant sign makes the space legally
> unenforceable.

### Plus a second, unrelated Texas sign — Tex. Occ. Code §2308.301

Distinct requirement, distinct statute, **covers the whole lot, not just accessible spaces**,
and **the RAS will not check it**. Required to legally tow anyone:

- **≥18" wide × 24" tall**, weather-resistant, permanently mounted
- **Bottom edge 5–8 ft above ground**
- International **towing symbol**
- States **who may park** and prohibits all others
- Bears **"Unauthorized Vehicles Will Be Towed or Booted at Owner's or Operator's Expense"**
- **24-hour telephone number with area code**
- At **each** driveway or curb cut; or where entrances are indefinite, at intervals so **no
  entrance is more than 25 ft from a sign**

> **Note for the record:** two independent research passes disagreed here — one found only
> §2308.301, the other only §68.104. **Both are required.** §68.104 is the TDLR accessibility
> rule the RAS inspects; §2308.301 is the towing statute that makes the lot enforceable.
> Neither substitutes for the other.

### Accessible parking counts — Table 208.2

1–25 spaces → 1 · 26–50 → 2 · 51–75 → 3 · 76–100 → 4 · 101–150 → 5 · 151–200 → 6 ·
201–300 → 7 · 301–400 → 8 · 401–500 → 9 · 501–1000 → 2% · 1001+ → 20 + 1 per 100 over 1000.

**Van spaces (§208.2.4):** ⌈accessible spaces ÷ 6⌉ — *"For every six or fraction of six"*.

⚠ **§208.2 Advisory:** the count is calculated **separately for each parking facility**, not
off the site total. If H2SEP has a front lot and a rear lot reading as separate facilities,
compute each independently — this yields **more** accessible spaces and is a frequent RAS
citation. **G001 carries the parking matrix and has not been extracted** (see
`PROJECT-FINDINGS.md` §1) — pull it before fixing the count.

## Tactile room identification — the largest package

Applies to every permanent room and space (§216.2 → 703.1, 703.2, 703.5; 703.2 pulls in
703.3, so **Braille is mandatory** even though 216.2 doesn't cite it directly).

**Character specs (§703.2):** raised **1/32" min** · **uppercase only** · **sans serif**,
not italic/script/decorative · "O" width **55%–110%** of "I" height · height **5/8"–2"**
(may drop to 1/2" min where separate raised and visual characters carry the same message) ·
stroke **≤15%** of height · character spacing 1/8" min to 4× stroke max, **3/8" min** from
raised borders · line spacing **135%–170%**.

**Braille (§703.3):** **contracted Grade 2**, domed/rounded dots. Dot base dia.
0.059"–0.063" · dots in cell 0.090"–0.100" o.c. · adjacent cells 0.241"–0.300" o.c. · **dot
height 0.025"–0.037"** · cell-to-cell below 0.395"–0.400" o.c. Positioned **below** the
entire text, **3/8" min** from other tactile characters. Uppercase indicator only before the
first word of a sentence, proper nouns, individual letters, initials and acronyms.
**Braille must spell the whole message** — "ROOM 214", not "214".

**Mounting (§703.4):**

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Height | **48" min** to baseline of the **lowest** tactile character; **60" max** to baseline of the **highest**. *Not a centerline dimension — a common field error.* |
| Single door | **Latch side** |
| Pair, one active leaf | **Inactive leaf** |
| Pair, two active leafs | **Right of the right-hand door** |
| No wall at latch side | Nearest adjacent wall |
| Clear floor space | **18" × 18" min**, centered on the tactile characters, **beyond the arc of the door swing between closed and 45° open** |
| Push-side exception | Permitted on the push side of doors **with closers and without hold-opens** |

⚠ **Sign location (703.4.2) is the single most common real-world RAS write-up** — signs on
the door leaf instead of the latch wall, or without the 18"×18" clear space. Verify in field.

**Pictograms (§703.6):** field **6" min height**, non-glare, contrasting, with a tactile text
descriptor **directly below the field** — characters and Braille must **not** sit inside the
pictogram field.

**Visual-only signs (§703.5)** — directional, informational, wayfinding, rules of conduct,
occupant load: **no tactile, no Braille required.** 40" min AFF; stroke 10%–30%; character
height per Table 703.5.5 by viewing distance (5/8" at <72", scaling to 3"+ above 120"
baseline at ≥21 ft).

### Which H2SEP spaces get one

All 55 room-ID signs in `sign-schedule.md`, plus all 115 guest room doors. Back-of-house
carries a nuance: **TAS §203.9** limits employee work areas to §206.2.8, §207.1 and §215.3
only — §216 is not in that list, so signs identifying spaces *within* an employee work area
are arguably unscoped. **But doors opening off a public corridor are routinely required by
RAS inspectors. Recommendation: sign all 35.** The cost delta is trivial against a failed
inspection. (TDLR TM 2013-21 *Employee Work Areas* is the reference.)

## ⚠ Areas of refuge — the answer depends on an unresolved project conflict

The chain: TAS §207.1 defers accessible means of egress to the IBC → TAS §216.4.2/.3
signage triggers only if the IBC requires an AOR → **IBC 1009.3.3 Exception 2** waives areas
of refuge in buildings sprinklered throughout per **§903.3.1.1 or §903.3.1.2**.

**That waiver keys to NFPA 13. It does not extend to NFPA 13R.**

H2SEP's sprinkler standard is contested three ways (`conflicts` **B5.3**, OPEN): G002 note 8
says NFPA 13, G100 says the basis of design is **13R**, and the Eagle Pass FD–stamped shop
drawing says 13. **So the sprinkler conflict directly decides whether areas of refuge and
their signage are required.** Resolve B5.3 before pricing the egress sign package.

**If NFPA 13 (waiver applies) — three requirements still survive:**

| Item | Location | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| **Two-way communication system** | Elevator landing on each accessible floor above the level of exit discharge — **floors 2, 3, 4** | IBC 1009.8 |
| **Instructions sign at the device** | Adjacent to each two-way communication device: directions for use, how to summon assistance, and **written identification of the location**. Visual characters (703.5) | IBC 1009.8.2 |
| **AMOE directional signage** | At exits serving an accessible space that are not an accessible means of egress, **and at elevator landings** | IBC 1009.10 |

**If NFPA 13R (no waiver) —** add "AREA OF REFUGE" door signage with ISA, illuminated where
exit signs are illuminated, visual + raised + Braille per IBC 1013.4 (IBC 1009.9), plus
posted instructions per IBC 1009.11.

## Restrooms — the counterintuitive new-construction answer

| Sign | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Tactile room identification | **Always required** (216.2) — 019 WOMENS · 020 MEN'S · 027 Unisex |
| **ISA on the restroom sign** | **NOT required in new construction.** §216.8 is conditioned entirely on *"Where **existing** toilet rooms or bathing rooms do not comply with 603."* TAS §213.2 requires every new-construction toilet room to comply with 603 → **the trigger never fires.** |
| Directional signs to an accessible restroom | **NOT required in new construction** — same conditional trigger |
| Unisex / family / all-gender | ADA/TAS impose **no special signage requirement**. Tactile + Braille ID per 216.2 is all. |
| Clustered single-user restrooms | *If* you use the §213.2 Exc. 4 allowance (only 50% of a cluster must comply), the complying rooms **must** be ISA-identified |
| ISA graphic form | Must be **Figure 703.7.2.1** — the classic static wheelchair symbol. Neither the Access Board nor TDLR has adopted the "Dynamic/Accessible Icon." **Do not specify the dynamic icon on a TDLR-inspected project.** |

**Practical recommendation:** put the ISA on anyway. It costs nothing, guests expect it, and
Hilton brand standards will likely require it. The point is that **if an RAS cites you for a
missing ISA on a new-construction restroom sign, the standard is on your side.**

## Guest rooms — 115 keys

**Every guest room door needs a tactile + Grade 2 Braille number sign.** §216.2's only
exception covers exterior signs not at the door they serve. §216.1 Exc. 1 exempts *"building
directories, menus, seat and row designations, occupant names, building addresses, and
company names and logos"* — a room number is none of those. (An occupant *name* plate would
be exempt; the number would not.)

| Item | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Sign identifying a room as **accessible** | **NOT REQUIRED.** §224 and §806 contain **no signage provision.** No ADA/TAS requirement to label a room "accessible" or "has communication features". |
| Communication-features hardware (not a sign) | §806.3.2 — visible notification for **incoming phone calls and a door knock or bell**, **not connected to the visible alarm appliances**; telephone volume control per 704.3; **electrical outlet within 48 inches of the telephone** to facilitate TTY use |
| **The real obligation is operational** | **28 CFR §36.302(e)** — the hotel must identify and describe accessible features **in enough detail for a guest to independently assess** whether a room meets their needs, allow reservation of **specific** accessible rooms, block them from general inventory, and hold them until all rooms of that type are rented. **This is the #1 source of hotel ADA litigation** and it is a policy requirement, not a sign. |

### Dispersion (§224.5) — easy to get wrong

1. Accessible rooms must be **dispersed among the various classes** of guest room, offering
   comparable choice of type, bed count and amenities.
2. Where the count is too small for full dispersion, prioritize **(1) room type, (2) number
   of beds, (3) amenities**.
3. **At least one** mobility-features room must **also** have communication features.
4. ⚠ **Not more than 10% of the mobility rooms may count toward the communication-features
   minimum.** For H2SEP: 10% of 7 = 0.7 → **1 room** may double-count. The other **11**
   communication-features rooms must be **separate** from the 7 mobility keys.

**H2SEP distinct accessible rooms: 7 mobility + 11 communication-only = 18 keys.** Check that
against the design — the room map identifies 8 of the 12 hearing rooms (209, 213, 216, 218,
309, 313, 316, 318) and **none of the 8 is one of the 7 mobility keys**, which is consistent,
but floors 1 and 4 have never been scanned. See `PROJECT-FINDINGS.md` §6.

## Amenities — what needs no sign

| Space | ADA/TAS signage |
|---|---|
| **Pool (ZONE-B)** | **NONE.** §242 and §1009 contain no signage provisions at all — the requirement is the accessible means of entry itself. §242.2 needs two means of entry, but **Exception 1**: a pool with **<300 linear feet of pool wall** needs only **one**, and it must be a **pool lift (1009.2) or sloped entry (1009.3)**. H2SEP's pool is well under 300 lf → **the fixed pool lift satisfies §242.2.** *(Texas DSHS pool signs are a separate law — see the pool file.)* |
| Fitness 023 | Tactile room ID only. §236 governs exercise machines — no signage. |
| Guest Laundry 024 | Tactile room ID only. §214 governs washers/dryers — no signage. |
| Breakfast 006 / Servery 009 | Tactile room ID only. §226 dining surfaces — no signage. |
| Meeting Room 018 | Tactile room ID + **assistive listening sign only if the room has permanently installed audio amplification** — §219.2 Exception: *"assistive listening systems shall not be required where audio amplification is not provided."* **Confirm whether 018 has installed amplification; this alone decides it.** |

**If ALS is required:** sign per **§216.10** with the **International Symbol of Access for
Hearing Loss** (Fig. 703.7.2.4), visual characters (703.5). Receiver count per Table 219.3 —
≤50 seats: 2 receivers (2 hearing-aid compatible); 51–200: **2 + 1 per 25 seats over 50**,
min 2 HAC; overall **25% of receivers, never fewer than 2**, must be hearing-aid compatible.

## Explicitly NOT required — don't over-buy

§216.1 exempts entirely: **building directories** (the Home2 lobby directory) · menus · seat
and row designations · **occupant names** · **building addresses** · **company names and
logos** · **temporary signs posted 7 days or less** · and in **parking facilities**, §216.2,
216.3 and 216.6–216.12 (216.5 → 502.6 still applies).

> The moment you add a permanent tactile label to a specific room, §216.2 governs it again.
