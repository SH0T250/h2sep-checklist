# City of Eagle Pass — signage requirements

_H2SEP · 3386 E. Main Street, Eagle Pass, TX 78852 (Maverick County). Researched 2026-08-20._

## 1. ⚠ The adopted code edition is genuinely contested — both answers are sourced

| Source | Says | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| **Codified ordinance** (§§ 8-15, 12-147, 13-32, 21-1) | **2015** I-Codes · **2014** NEC | Read directly from the eLaws mirror |
| **City's own current permit forms + Fire Marshal plan review** (rev. 06/2025) | **2018** ICC · **2017** NEC · **2012 TAS** | Extracted from the City's PDFs |

**Ord. No. 2016-13 (2016-03-22)** is the omnibus that moved Eagle Pass from 2012 to 2015
I-Codes. **A successor ordinance adopting the 2018 codes very likely exists but could not be
located online — do not guess its number.** Municode serves only an empty shell to automated
fetching; the eLaws mirror carries the same codified text but its supplement history **ends at
Supplement 29 / Ord. 2019-08 (2019-04-02)**, so anything adopted after April 2019 is invisible
in the text available.

**Practical resolution:** build to **2018** — it is what the Fire Marshal's current plan-review
document and the June 2025 permit applications say, and it is the more recent cycle. But **get
the adopting ordinance number in writing** before anyone cites an edition formally.

**Good news for signage: it barely matters.** The provisions that drive sign fabrication —
IFC 505.1 address characters, Appendix D fire lane signs, IFC 1013/1023 egress marking — are
**identical in the 2015 and 2018 editions**. The conflict matters for other trades, not for the
sign package.

**The City names the 2012 Texas Accessibility Standards on its own permit application** — the
standard G401 does not cite. See `CBC-vs-TAS-DELTA.md`.

## 2. ✅ Appendix D is adopted — the fire lane sign spec is law here

This closes the fire package's top open question. **Verified verbatim** from § 13-32(Q):

> *"Appendix A Board of Appeals is not adopted, Appendix B Fire Flow Requirements for Buildings
> is adopted, Appendix C Fire Hydrant Locations is not adopted, **Appendix D Fire Apparatus
> Access Roads is adopted**; Appendix E Hazard Categories is adopted, Appendix F Hazard Ranking
> is adopted… Appendix I Fire Protection Systems; Non-Compliant Conditions is adopted, **Appendix
> J Building Information Sign is not adopted**…"*

So:

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| **Fire lane signs — 12 in. wide × 18 in. high, red letters on white reflective, "NO PARKING—FIRE LANE"** | **Enforceable.** IFC D103.6, adopted |
| Both sides of lanes 20–26 ft wide; one side of lanes >26 and <32 ft | **Enforceable.** D103.6.1, D103.6.2 |
| **Sign spacing interval** | ⚠ **Appendix D sets none.** No Eagle Pass standard published. **Ask the Fire Marshal** |
| **Curb paint color / stenciled curb lettering** | ⚠ **No Eagle Pass ordinance found.** Red curb + white stencil is a neighboring-jurisdiction convention, **not** an adopted Eagle Pass standard. **Ask** |
| **NFPA-704-style "Building Information Sign"** | **Not required** — Appendix J expressly **not** adopted |

**Local amendment, stricter than base code** (§ 13-32(F)(1)(o)): *"Indicate the location of the
building address placard, which shall be **legible from both the street and fire lane**."* Base
IFC 505.1 only requires visibility from the street. **Eagle Pass wants it readable from both.**

⚠ **Correction to our brief.** We assumed **Tex. Transp. Code § 545.3051** was a fire lane
signage statute. It is not — it is *"Removal of Personal Property from Roadway or
Right-of-way,"* and imposes **no signage duty on a hotel**. The tow-signage statute is
**Tex. Occ. Code Ch. 2308** (see `ADA-TAS-REQUIREMENTS.md` §2308.301).

## 3. ⚠⚠ The sign ordinance will not fit a standard Home2 package

**Appendix A § 15**, "Regulations for placement of signs and billboards" — Ord. No. 06-13 (2006),
amended Ord. No. 07-15 (2007). There is **no standalone sign chapter**; signage lives entirely
in the zoning appendix, and there is **no separate sign permit form** — it is a checkbox on the
Commercial Building Permit Application.

**Verified verbatim:**

> *"Each business establishment shall be allowed to erect and maintain **two (2) permanent type
> signs** for the advertising of its services or products."*
>
> *"Only **one (1) free-standing sign** located adjacent to a public street will be permitted for
> each legal lot."*
>
> *"The aggregate (total) area of the signs shall not exceed **two hundred fifty (250) feet per
> legal lot**. Free standing signs shall not exceed an amount equal to **one and one-half (1.5)
> square feet for each one (1) linear foot of the lot's frontage** abutting the primary street,
> except that a **minimum of forty-eight (48) square feet** of sign area is allowed regardless of
> the lot frontage."*
>
> *"In no event shall any portion of any sign extend to a height of more than **thirty-five (35)
> feet** above the surface of the ground."*
>
> *"No sign or any part thereof shall be located within **ten (10) feet of any property line**"*
> — with stricter requirements at intersections.

| Rule | Limit | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent signs per establishment | **2** (+1 more if the establishment fronts two streets, one per street) | § 15(h)(1), (1)(A) |
| Free-standing signs | **1 per legal lot** | § 15(h)(3) |
| Aggregate sign area | **250 sq ft per legal lot**, all signs combined | § 15(h)(1)(A) |
| Free-standing area formula | **1.5 sq ft per linear ft of primary-street frontage**, min 48 sq ft | § 15(h)(1)(A) |
| Double-sided panel | Counts as **one** sign | § 15(h)(1)(B) |
| Max height | **35 ft** | § 15(h)(B), (h)(1)(C) |
| Setback — any property line | **10 ft** | § 15(h)(C) |
| Setback — intersecting street | **25 ft** | § 15(h)(C) |
| Setback — right-of-way lines | **15 ft** — apply the greater of this and the 10 ft rule | § 15(h)(1)(D) |
| Signs in ROW | **Prohibited**, including overhead projections | § 15(h)(2) |
| Canopy / porte-cochère signs | May not extend above the roof line by more than **2 ft** | § 15(b) |
| Where IBC and § 15 conflict | **§ 15 controls** | § 15(j) |
| Permit fee | **$88.00** | Commercial permit application, rev. 06/2025 |
| Penalty | Misdemeanor, up to **$200**, each day a separate offense | § 15(n) |
| Appeal | **ZBAA within 30 days**; variance only where *"the unusual shape or topography of the property prevents signage allowable under this chapter from adequately identifying the business"* | § 15(e) |

### The collision

A standard Home2 exterior package is **two wall signs + one monument = three.** The ordinance
allows **two.** The only relief is the two-street exception or a ZBAA variance — and the
variance standard is narrow (unusual shape or topography), not "the brand requires it."

**And dropping a brand ID sign needs Hilton's written consent as well as the City's** — the
Franchise Agreement's definition of "Designs" expressly includes signs, and approved plans
cannot change without further written consent. **This is a long-lead approval on two fronts,
not a field decision.** See `BRAND-AND-PROJECT-RECORD.md`.

### 🚫 No digital or animated signage

§ 15(k)(J) bans *"visible moving, revolving, or rotating parts… or other apparent visible
movement achieved by electrical, electronic, or mechanical means, **except for
time/temperature/date signs**."* § 15(k)(K) bans lights that *"flash, move, rotate, scintillate,
blink, flicker, **vary in intensity or color**, or use intermittent electrical pulsations."*

**An LED reader board or EMC is effectively prohibited.** "Vary in intensity" is broad enough to
reach some dimming schemes — confirm any adaptive-brightness driver with the City.

### The permit-exemption ambiguity

§ 15(g)(F) exempts from permit: *"Flat signs of solid-face construction that are placed against
and attached to the vertical wall of a building… **may not project beyond the wall to which they
are attached**, and must be attached at least ten (10) feet above ground level."* But § 15(h)(A)
requires a permit for **all** on-premise signs.

**Hilton channel letters are stood off the wall, so they likely do project beyond it and the
exemption probably does not apply.** Genuinely ambiguous — **get the City's answer in writing.**

### ⚠ The wind-load table is missing from the code

§ 15(h)(D) requires a **certificate signed by the sign owner** that any sign **6 ft or more above
grade** will withstand wind-load pressures *"as set out in the following table."* **That table
does not appear in the codified text.** Eagle Pass is a high-wind border region. **Ask what PSF
value they enforce and whether a sealed engineer's letter is accepted in place of an owner's
certificate.**

## 5. ⚠ Historic district — probably not, but unverified

Eagle Pass has a historic overlay: **Ch. 8 Art. IX**, Ord. No. 2012-35 (2012-11-06), administered
by the **Main Street Advisory Board**. A **Certificate of Appropriateness** is required inside the
district for *"any new construction of a principal building or accessory building or structure
subject to view from a public way."* The City's permit applications list COA as an "Other
Required Inspection (when applicable)."

**§ 8-184(d) requires the boundary map to be posted on the city website. It is not there.** The
Main Street district is the downtown corridor near the international bridge (low Main St.
numbers); **3386 E. Main is roughly 3 miles east and very probably outside** — but that could not
be verified against the actual map. **A COA requirement would add a design-review body over the
Home2 exterior signage package, on top of the City and Hilton.** Confirm with the Main Street
Manager before finalizing sign design.

## 6. TxDOT — on-premise signs need no state permit

Main Street in Eagle Pass carries **Business US 277-N**, a TxDOT route (and TxDOT's own Maverick
County Maintenance office is at 2440 Main St.). **Whether the designation reaches the 3386 block
could not be confirmed** — BU 277-N is only ~3.2 certified miles.

- **On-premise signs need no TxDOT permit.** *"A permit… is not required to erect an on-premise
  sign"* — 43 TAC § 21.442. TxDOT's Commercial Signs Regulatory Program regulates **off-premise**
  outdoor advertising only.
- **Nothing may sit in state ROW** — and § 15(h)(2) independently bars any sign or overhead
  projection in public ROW. Confirm no monument footing or landscape lighting encroaches.
- A **TxDOT access/driveway permit** is required for any connection to a state-maintained highway.

## 8. What could not be verified — exact questions to ask

**Planning — (830) 773-7781 / planning@eaglepasstx.gov**

1. **What ordinance adopted the 2018 I-Codes and 2017 NEC?** The codified §§ 8-15, 12-147, 13-32,
   21-1 still say 2015/2014, but your 06/2025 permit forms say 2018/2017. **Send the adopting
   ordinance PDF.** Which edition governs a permit applied for today?
2. **Is Appendix A § 15 still the operative sign ordinance, or amended since April 2019?**
3. **What is the zoning district of the parcel, and is it inside city limits?** The posted zoning
   map link (`DocumentCenter/View/1768/City-Zoning-Map`) **returns 404**.
4. **Is 3386 E. Main inside the Historic Preservation District?** § 8-184(d) requires the boundary
   map be posted; it is not. **If yes, does exterior signage need a COA, and on what schedule?**
5. **Do flush-mounted internally-illuminated channel letters need a sign permit?** § 15(g)(F) vs
   § 15(h)(A).
6. **Is the $88 fee per sign or per application?**
7. **How do you count signs against the two-sign limit?** Does a multi-elevation wall package
   count as one or several? Does the property qualify as fronting two streets? **If we exceed two
   signs or 250 sq ft, is a ZBAA variance the only path — and what is the fee and hearing
   calendar?**
8. **What wind-load table applies?** § 15(h)(D) references a table that is not in the code. Will
   you accept a sealed engineer's letter instead of an owner's certificate?
9. **What is the lot's linear frontage on the primary street?** Needed for the 1.5 sq ft/ft
   free-standing allowance.

**Fire Marshal — (830) 773-1915 / ecuevas@eaglepasstx.gov**

10. **What fire lane sign SPACING interval do you require?** Appendix D sets size, color, wording
    and which side — but no interval.
11. **Do you require painted curb striping, and if so what color, stencil wording, letter height
    and interval?** No ordinance specifies it.
12. **Do you want "TOW AWAY ZONE" on the fire lane signs, or is Occ. Code Ch. 2308 signage handled
    separately at the drive entrances?**
13. **Exact requirements for the address placard "legible from both the street and fire lane"** —
    character height, stroke, contrast, **and must it be illuminated?** How many, and where?
14. **Which IFC edition are you enforcing — 2015 or 2018?**
15. Plus the fire package's remaining items — see `FIRE-MARSHAL-SIGNAGE.md` §10.

**TxDOT — (830) 773-2617**

16. **Is E. Main at the 3386 block state-maintained?** If so, where is the ROW line, and does
    anything in the sign package encroach?

**Not verifiable online at all:** the § 15(h)(D) wind-load table · the historic district boundary
