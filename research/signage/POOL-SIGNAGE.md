# Pool signage and markings — H2SEP outdoor guest pool (ZONE-B)

_Texas state rules. Researched 2026-08-20. **The sign wording and letter heights in §2 and §4
below were extracted directly from DSHS's own published sign-requirements PDF**, not
paraphrased from a secondary source. City of Eagle Pass local rules are in the city file;
under 25 TAC §265.181(g) local authorities may adopt standards **equal to or more stringent**
than the state rule, so local review can only add._

## 1. Threshold findings — these drive everything else

| Question | Finding | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Is it a "public pool"? | **Yes** — Texas "public pool" includes hotel pools regardless of fee | 25 TAC §265.182(51) |
| **Class** | **CLASS C** — *"operated for and in conjunction with lodging, such as hotels, motels…"* | §265.182(51)(C)(i) |
| Lifeguard required? | **No.** Class C is not on the mandatory-lifeguard list | §265.191(a)(1)–(5) |
| ⚠ **Class-creep trap** | If the hotel ever **sells day passes or opens the pool to the public**, it must meet **Class B** operational *and lifeguard* standards while open — **two lifeguards**. Same trap if a **youth group, youth camp, childcare group or visiting school group** swims. | §265.194(a); §265.191(a) |
| Does HSC Ch. 757 (Pool Yard Enclosures) apply? | **No.** §757.001 defines "multiunit rental complex" and **expressly excludes "a facility primarily renting rooms to overnight guests."** Hotels are carved out. Enclosure is governed by **25 TAC §265.192(d)** instead. | HSC §757.001–.002 |
| Governing version | 25 TAC Ch. 265 Subch. L **as amended eff. 5/2/2024**. ⚠ **Section numbers moved twice** — see §7 | 49 TexReg 2695 |
| Design code adopted by reference | **2021 ISPSC** Chs. 2–6 + §102, **except §412.2, §508.3, §603.3**; plus ANSI/APSP-16 and ANSI/PHTA/ICC-7 | §265.181(b)–(d) |
| Enforcement | DSHS **or** the local regulatory authority | §265.181(g), §265.197 |

**Panel rules to hand the sign vendor, verbatim:**

> *"Signs shall be in the pool yard, securely mounted as applicable, and readily visible to the
> pool user."* · *"Sign panels shall be durable for the weather conditions. The message surface
> shall be clean and smooth and shall readily accept paint or precut lettering adhesives."* ·
> ***"Theming or artwork applied to signs shall not invade the message panel. Signs shall have a
> distinct border."*** · *"Multiple signs may be used, or the messages may be combined on one
> sign."*

⚠ **Substitute wording needs pre-approval.** Variations are allowed **only** if *"substantially
equivalent"* **and** local regulatory officials **approve the variation before the sign is
posted**. If the vendor proposes anything other than the strings above, get that approval in
writing first — a stock "Texas pool sign" off a catalog is not automatically compliant.

**Spanish is permitted, not required.** *"In areas of Texas where the majority of citizens are
non-English speaking, in addition to signs in English, signs and other written warnings or
information required by these standards may be posted in the predominant language."* Eagle Pass
is a majority Spanish-speaking community — **bilingual signage is recommended as a risk
measure**; confirm whether the City requires it.

Also required if the emergency device is present: a sign **directly above** it giving the
precise location of the pool (address, building number, GPS), minimum **1 inch**.

## 3. Depth markers and NO DIVING — the TL-12 tile scope

⚠ **This is the section that must be dimensionally correct, because on this project the depth
markers are specified as TILE — tag TL-12 on ID-1.8 — not as applied signage.** Two rule sets
stack (25 TAC §265.190(b)–(c) and ISPSC 2021 §409); comply with the more stringent of each.

| Requirement | Spec |
|---|---|
| Quantity | **Not fewer than two per pool**, regardless of size or shape |
| Character height | **No less than 4 inches**, numbers **and** letters |
| Color | **Permanent**, in a color **contrasting** the background of the deck **and** the vertical wall |
| Wall placement | In the **top 4½ inches of the pool wall just under the coping**, positioned to be **read by a user while in the pool** |
| Deck placement | **Slip-resistant**, readable **while standing on the deck**; ISPSC adds **within 18 inches of the water edge**, facing the water |
| Spacing | **Uniformly around the perimeter at intervals of no more than 25 feet**; ISPSC adds: at **maximum and minimum depths**, at **all points of slope change**, and at **depth increments not exceeding 2 feet**, **uniformly distributed on all sides and ends** |
| Units | Must **spell out "feet"/"inches"** or abbreviate **"FT," "IN,"** or fractions of a foot |
| Accuracy | Within **± 3 inches** of actual depth at normal operating level (ISPSC §409.2) |

**NO DIVING on the deck:** required **where pool depth is 5 feet or less**, at intervals **not
greater than 25 feet** and **directly adjacent to a depth marker**. Symbol is *"a diver's
profile in a circle with a 45-degree slash through the diver"*; marker **and** symbol **not less
than 4 inches high**, permanent contrasting color, slip-resistant, within 18" of the water's
edge, read standing on the deck facing the water.

⚠ **The waterline tile band does not satisfy it.** *"The no diving marker and symbol is not
required on the interior tile line of a pool or in a spa"* — it is a **deck** marking. Also
required on **any permanent structure above the deck within 5 ft of the water surface** (exempt:
diving boards/platforms, **ADA-compliant chair lifts**, slide flumes, lifeguard stands, bridges).

**Transition-line tile:** only if the pool **exceeds 5 ft deep** — then a **4-inch minimum width
row of floor tile** in a color contrasting the pool bottom at the shallow-to-deep transition.

> **For the tile shop drawing:** if the pool is ≤5'-0" throughout (typical Home2 prototype),
> TL-12 must deliver 4-inch numerals with spelled or abbreviated units, on the wall in the top
> 4½" under the coping **and** on the deck within 18" of the water's edge, contrasting and
> permanent, slip-resistant on the deck, at ≤25 ft spacing plus every slope change and every
> 2 ft of depth change — **with a 4-inch NO DIVING word-mark and slashed-diver symbol on the
> deck adjacent to every depth marker.** No transition-line tile needed.

### ⚠ Correction — three spa warnings we assumed are not Texas requirements

The brief asked for elderly/pregnancy/heart-disease/diabetes/blood-pressure warnings, a
**15-minute limit**, and **"no unsupervised children under 5."** **None of those are Texas
requirements.**

- Texas uses **"PERSONS UNDER THE AGE OF 14"** — **do not post "under 5."**
- There is **no 15-minute time-limit sign** and **no medical-conditions sign** in the Texas
  figure or in ISPSC §508.2.
- Those come from other states' codes, ANSI/APSP-11, and spa-manufacturer literature. They are
  **permitted extras** — but crowding them onto a required panel risks violating the *"theming
  or artwork must not invade the message panel"* rule. **Put extras on a separate panel.**

## 5. Enclosure, gates, and the chemical room

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Enclosure height | **Minimum 48 inches**, measured from ground on the **outside** |
| Openings | Nothing that passes a **4-inch diameter sphere** |
| Climbability | **No horizontal mid-rail**; on a 48" fence, horizontal members **at least 45 inches apart**. **Chain link is prohibited** for Class C |
| Clear zone | No planters, light poles, structures or site furnishings **within 36 inches** horizontally outside the enclosure; tree limbs trimmed |
| Gates | **Self-closing and self-latching**, **opening outward away from the pool**, lockable, locked when closed |
| **Gate latch height** | Hardware **at least 3½ ft (42 in) above the deck**, **or** on the pool-yard side at least 3 inches below the top of the gate; **no opening greater than ½ inch within 18 inches of the latch** (including the gate-to-post gap) |
| Propping | No gate may be propped open unless an employee or contractor is present doing work that reasonably requires it |
| Pool-closed sign | Required at entry gates when the pool is closed |

### ⚠ The gate latch has a 6-inch compliant window on this project

25 TAC §265.192(d)(3)(A) sets a **42-inch minimum** latch height. **AS104 keyed note 37** —
already carried in the project data at **HIGH** reliability — requires **"Maximum 48" AFF to any
operable part at the pool"** (the ADA reach range).

**So the latch must land between 42" and 48" AFF.** Many self-latching pool gate latches default
to a top-of-gate mount that overshoots 48". **Verify the specified gate hardware set achieves
both before it is ordered.**

### ⚠ The pool building may not be able to open both ways

Per §265.192(d)(1)(F), if a room forms part of the pool yard enclosure, its door may open into
the pool yard **only if that same room has no door or gate opening to the outside of the
enclosure.**

**Room 035 Pool Equipment has a D3 pair of doors** (A600, HIGH). If a pair opens outward for
equipment access **and** another door opens into the pool yard, that is a violation — and a
direct route for a child into the pool yard. **Verify on AS104.** *(Service gates used only by
service personnel are exempt from self-closing/self-latching, but must never be used as user
entry and must be kept closed and locked when not in actual use.)*

### Chemical room — 034 / 035 / 036

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Unauthorized-entry sign** | *"A warning sign against unauthorized entry must be posted on the entry door or gate to the pool and spa equipment room, building, or area."* ⚠ **The rule prescribes neither wording nor letter height.** "AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY" is the conventional compliant text — **do not claim a mandated wording.** (§265.194(g)) |
| **NFPA 704 hazard diamond** | IFC §5003.5 requires it at entrances where hazardous materials are stored **"in quantities requiring a permit"** *or* **at locations designated by the fire code official** |
| NFPA 704 values | Must come from the **SDS of the actual products stored**, reflecting the **most hazardous** material present. Bottom quadrant recognizes only **OX**, **W̶** and **SA** — calcium hypochlorite is **OX**; muriatic acid takes **no** special symbol. ⚠ **"COR" is not a valid NFPA 704 symbol** (that is HMIS/DOT) |
| SDS | *"Safety data sheets covering all chemicals for which personnel are responsible must be kept on-site and be readily available."* (§265.189(f); also IFC §5003.4) |
| Container labeling | *"All chemical bulk and day tanks must be clearly labeled to indicate the tank's contents"* (§265.189(h)); plus IFC §5003.5.1 and OSHA HazCom 29 CFR 1910.1200(f) |
| Physical storage | Inaccessible to users; **dry chemicals off the floor** or in waterproof containers in a dry room; protected from flooding or wetting from floors, walls and ceiling; **chlorine compounds must NOT be stored with petroleum products**; **compressed chlorine gas is prohibited** in pools built on or after 1/1/2021 (§265.189(e),(i)) |

⚠ **Honest read on the NFPA 704 diamond:** IFC §5003.5 triggers on permit-quantity thresholds
(Table 105.5.22), and a small hotel pool room holding a few drums may fall **below** them — the
numeric thresholds could not be retrieved. **But Texas fire marshals routinely require the
diamond on pool chemical rooms under the "designated by the fire code official" clause, and it
is a near-zero-cost item. Install it — and get the specific ratings confirmed in writing by the
Eagle Pass Fire Marshal against the actual SDS for the chemicals the pool contractor will stock.
Do not let anyone print it from a generic template.**

## 6. Rescue equipment, maximum user load, and things that are NOT required

**Rescue equipment** (§265.190(f)) — visible and readily accessible from all areas of the pool
yard: at least **one ring buoy with throwing rope and one reaching pole per 2,000 sq ft** of
pool surface up to 6,000 sq ft, plus one more set per additional 4,000 sq ft or fraction. Ring
buoy **USCG-approved, max 24-inch diameter**; rope **¼"–⅜" diameter, length ≥ ⅔ the maximum
width of the pool**. Reaching pole: *"light, strong, non-telescoping, and at least 12 feet
long… constructed of fiberglass or other material that does not conduct electricity"* with a
**shepherd's crook with blunted ends**.

**Emergency summoning device** — within **200 feet of the water**, readily accessible and
functioning whenever the pool is open; **must not be answered by an on-site office**; must reach
911; clear operating instructions provided.

**Maximum user load** is posted but the formula lives in **ISPSC Table 403.1**, driven by deck
area relative to water surface area:

| Deck condition | Shallow area | Deep area |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum deck area | 15 sq ft/user | 20 sq ft/user |
| Deck ≥ water surface area | 12 sq ft/user | 15 sq ft/user |
| Deck ≥ 2× water surface area | 8 sq ft/user | 10 sq ft/user |

**Action:** have the pool designer state the computed load on AS104. **A generous patio (ZONE-B)
can move the pool into the 8/10 column and materially raise the posted number.**

### Do not over-build — these are NOT Texas requirements

| Asked about | Status |
|---|---|
| "NO RUNNING" | **Not required** — not in the figure |
| "SHOWER BEFORE ENTERING" | **Not required** — a Model Aquatic Health Code *recommendation* only |
| "NO FOOD OR DRINK" | **No sign required.** §265.194(p) prohibits consumption in the pool and (q) bans glass in the pool yard — only the **glass** message is a required sign |
| "No swimming under the influence" | **Not required** |
| Spa medical warnings / 15-minute limit / "under 5" | **Not required** — see §4 |
| **First aid kit, AED, backboard at the pool** | **Not required** at an unguarded Class C pool — §265.191(k) is lifeguard-scoped |
| **Pool lift sign** | **Not required** by ADA §242/§1009 — no signage provision exists |
| **CPO certificate posted** | **Not required to be posted.** A certified operator is required, but need not be on-site; name and contact must be *"made available"* — the rule never says "posted" |
| **State pool permit posted** | **No state permit exists.** Check local |
| VGBA signage | **None.** VGBA is a product-marking law — drain covers must meet ANSI/APSP-16 and carry its permanent markings including **flow rating in GPM**. Documentation kept on-site, not posted (§265.194(e)) |

**ADA:** pool wall **< 300 linear feet → one** accessible means of entry, which must be a **pool
lift or sloped entry**. *"A pool lift must remain in place and be operational during all times
that the pool is open"* — **storage-and-retrieval-on-request is prohibited**, which supports the
fixed lift on this project.

## 7. ⚠ Section numbering moved twice — cite carefully

| Topic | **Current (eff. 5/2/2024)** | 1/1/2023 | 1/1/2021 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pool safety sign figure | **§265.190(e)(5)** | §265.190(f)(5) | §265.201(j)(5) |
| Spa safety sign figure | **§265.190(g)(1)** | §265.190(h)(4) | §265.208(e) |
| Depth markers | **§265.190(b)** | §265.190(b)–(c) | §265.201 |
| Deck NO DIVING | **§265.190(c)** | §265.190(d) | §265.201 |
| Rescue equipment | **§265.190(f)** | §265.190(g) | — |

**DSHS's own hosted PDFs are out of date on numbering** — the rule PDF is the 1/1/2023 text and
the sign handout is the 1/1/2021 text (it is the handout the wording in §2 and §4 was extracted
from). **Their substantive sign wording and letter heights are unchanged and reliable.** The
5/2/2024 amendment's only substantive change was clarifying that the "under 14" sign applies
where a lifeguard is **not** required or provided — which on this project means **it is
required**. Expect an inspector to be working from the 2023 checklist numbering.
