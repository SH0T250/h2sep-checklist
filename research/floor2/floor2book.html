How it was built

The same recipe that built floor 1 and the four mock-ups, proved byte for byte against the floor-1 generator on every run. A donor may enrich a line; it may not launder it: this room's own reliability, own citation and own marks govern, and every line says in a SOURCE sentence where each part of it came from.
