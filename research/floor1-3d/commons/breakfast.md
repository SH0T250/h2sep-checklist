## Basis

Every position is SCALED: pixel positions on the A513/A514 split PNGs mapped onto the MEASURED A100 walls (room widths read back within 0.2 ft). Millwork, dishmachine and oven sizes are MEASURED from the RK shop drawings and the submittals. Counter-top appliance and fixture footprints are SCALED from the plan symbols because the 17 MB Clevenger cut-sheet package is over the Drive download limit; their heights are left null.
