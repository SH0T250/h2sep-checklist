# Commons kit: lobby (Lobby 003, Market 005, Reception 004, Vestibule 001)

Companion to `lobby.json`. Every position is in the A100 frame (U feet from the north face, V feet from the west face). The pieces come from ID-1.7, the Enlarged Lobby Furniture and Equipment Plan (RK Hospitality, 07/04/2025, 3/16 in = 1 ft), read from the split sheet `FF&E Locations_sheet8.pdf`. The sheet was fitted to the A100 frame with the south exterior face (U 246.46) and the east face (V 65.59) at 13.5 pt per foot; the four interior faces of Fitness 023 then land within 0.1 ft of the A100 values, so placements read off the sheet are good to about half a foot.

## Judgment calls and gaps

- Reception desk plan symbol versus shop drawing differ (see above); the shop drawing governs.
- Focus booth 2 (south) is drawn 58 in long against the 80 in spec; the second PA-303 tea table is not clearly drawn and is placed at low confidence.
- The PA-100 banquette spine is drawn narrower than 52 in; the tag leader fixes its center.
- No fireplace, planters, feature-wall millwork or breakfast host stand appears on ID-1.7. TV lounge pieces (PA-108, PA-113, PA-305, PA-308) carry no tags in the lobby.
- PA-316 height, PA-505 size, PA-307 dimensions and PA-306 height did not come through the spec text layer; assumed heights are labeled in the JSON.
- Lighting and the ACB-001 baffle ceiling over the desk are left to the lighting kit.

Sources with Drive ids are listed in `lobby.json`.
