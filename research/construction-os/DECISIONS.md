| D16 | 2026-08-19 | **Incident, fixed: a merge write wiped 46 contacts in the cloud.** Saving a contact edit in the app called `createIfMissing`, which used `setDoc(..., {merge: true})` with an empty `items: {}` in the skeleton. An empty map is a leaf in Firestore's merge field mask, so it wrote the empty map OVER the live one, and the only record left was the one being saved. Found within the hour by the sync tool's diff. Fixed at the cause: `createIfMissing` now reads first and writes only into a genuinely empty slot, and `ensureDoc` returns early whenever a copy already exists. Both guards are covered by a regression test. Restored from the sheet, 49 contacts verified, Austin's in-app edit preserved. No room, punch, or assignment data was touched; every room doc kept its exact updateTime through the whole episode. Standing lesson: never merge a skeleton over a document that can already hold records. |
