For the record

Drawing defects found while building this report

  • The COMcheck energy certificates (E800/E801) belong to a different building. Every page of both embedded reports is titled "Bailey's Forest Event Center," Cornelius NC — another MWT job — with that building's areas and fixture counts. They are on this project's permit record and should be reissued by MWT. (They are also why no COMcheck fixture count is used anywhere above.)
  • E101's electrician guarantee explicitly excludes lamps ("LAMPS ARE EXCLUDED FROM THIS GUARANTEE") — consistent with the owner buying bulbs, and worth knowing at turnover.
  • E400 note: bathroom switches carry integrated night-lights (typ.) — a lamped device inside the switch, no separate bulb.
