## Detail per tag

### PA-201 — Pendant, Focus Booths — COUNT: 2 (HIGH confidence)
- **ID-1.6 (Enlarged Lobby RCP, 3/16" = 1'-0")** carries exactly two `PA-201` tags. Verified on the text layer (only two occurrences of the string on the sheet; Drive full-text confirms PA-201 appears on no other sheet in the 41-sheet set) and visually at 5x zoom.
  - Tag 1: west side of Lobby 003, north booth position (across from the Womens 019 wall) — boxed label `PA-201`, single leader terminating on **one** large circle-with-crosshair pendant symbol (~30 pt dia = ~27" at 3/16" scale, consistent with the 27" bell spec) adjacent to the wall/column at the booth.
  - Tag 2: southwest corner of Lobby 003 near Vestibule 001 — identical boxed label, single leader to **one** identical pendant symbol.
- No "(typ.)" note, no parenthetical quantity, no multi-headed leaders. A sweep of the whole lobby zone at mid-zoom found **no additional untagged symbols of this size** (the small dots are downlights on the architect's grid).
- Booth locations line up with the two PA-105 (booth sofa) + PA-306 (booth table) clusters on the west wall of ID-1.7 — i.e., the two Focus Booths.
- **Cross-check A120 (arch first-floor RCP):** small crossed-circle pendant symbols appear at both booth positions by Womens 019 / Men's 020. Consistent.
- **Bulbs: 2 fixtures × 1 lamp = 2 × A15 LED E26 3000K 11W.**

### PA-205 — Pendant, Reception — COUNT: 3 (HIGH confidence)
- **ID-1.6** carries exactly **one** `PA-205` tag (only occurrence in the entire set per Drive full-text). Boxed label south-west of Reception 004 with a single leader running up into the reception slat feature ceiling (ACB-01, 8'-10" AFF).
- The leader terminates on the **leftmost of a row of three identical circle-with-crosshair pendant symbols**, evenly spaced east-west over the reception desk. Verified at 5x zoom across the full width of the slat ceiling: exactly 3 symbols, no more.
- Printed annotations: **none** — no "(3)", no "typ." The count of 3 is a **visual symbol count**, not a printed quantity.
- **Cross-check A120:** three crossed-circle pendant symbols in the same row directly below the RECEPTION 004 label. Matches.
- **Bulbs: 3 fixtures × 3 lamps = 9 × A15 E26 3000K 11W.** (If the spec-book "3 lamps" were ever intended to describe the 3-pendant group rather than lamping per pendant, the total would be 3 — the drawings cannot resolve that; the spec book governs. As drawn: 3 discrete pendants.)

### PA-200 — Perch Table Lamp — COUNT: 1 tagged placement (MODERATE confidence) — FLAGGED
- **ID-1.7 (Enlarged Lobby F&E Plan)** carries exactly **one** `PA-200` tag (only occurrence in the whole drawing set), in the tag stack west of the lobby communal work table: `PA-304 (2)` / `PA-200` / `PA-803`. Single leader terminating at the **west end of the communal work table** (the racetrack table with the center power/accessory trough, PA-300 (3) chairs).
- Printed quantity: **none.** By this sheet's own convention, multi-quantity items always print "(n)" after the tag (PA-103 (3), PA-300 (3)/(5), PA-304 (2), PA-303 (2), etc.) — so the un-annotated PA-200 reads as **quantity 1**.
- **Ambiguity noted for the record:** the tabletop trough carries six small circular symbols — one concentric-circle symbol at each end (the leader lands on the west one) and four smaller staggered circles along the run. Whether the east-end symbol is a second PA-200 or a power grommet is not determinable from this sheet; the middle four read as trough grommets/accessories. If designer intent was a lamp at each end, the count would be 2 — confirm with RKH only if a substitute product is being bought.
- **FLAG (as instructed):** spec book lists PA-200 as **"Product Deleted May-23"** — the tag was never removed from the 07/04/2025 plan. **Order zero bulbs**; carry the 1 tagged placement as a coordination item (either the tag dies, or a substitute lamp + its bulb get added by the designer).

### PA-203 — Floor Lamp @ TV Lounge — CANNOT BE COUNTED FROM THE SHEETS (open item)
- The string `PA-203` does **not appear anywhere** in the 41-sheet FF&E Locations set: not on ID-1.6, ID-1.7 (the primary public-area F&E sheet, whose text layer cleanly lists 40+ other PA tags), ID-1.10, ID-1.11, nor any other sheet (Drive full-text across the folder returns zero drawing hits; it appears only in spec-book-side files).
- Visual sweep of the entire ID-1.7 lobby/TV-lounge seating zone confirms: every tag in the lounge is legible and none reads PA-203. There is no zone bubble or leader for it either — the tag simply is not placed.
- **Conclusion: quantity is not derivable from the drawings.** Keep as an open item — quantity must come from the PA FF&E spec book order list or from RKH/purchasing agent.

### PA-206 — Table Lamp @ TV Lounge — CANNOT BE COUNTED FROM THE SHEETS (open item)
- Same finding as PA-203: `PA-206` appears on **no sheet** in the FF&E Locations set (text layer + Drive full-text + visual sweep of the lounge). Not placed on any plan.
- **Conclusion: open item — confirm quantity from spec book / designer.**

## Notes and caveats
1. **Counting basis:** ID-1.6/ID-1.7 text layers extract cleanly (dozens of other PA tags legible with coordinates), so absence of a tag string from the text layer is meaningful, and every present tag was verified visually at 5x zoom. Page rotation (270°) was accounted for when mapping text coordinates to crops.
2. **No printed quantities exist for any of the five tags** — PA-201/PA-205 quantities above are visual symbol counts; PA-200 is an inferred single unit from the sheet's "(n)" convention.
3. **A120 cross-check is corroborating only** — it places pendant symbols (legend "P — PENDANT") but tabulates no counts and carries no PA-2xx tags except PA-204 (corridor).
4. ID-1.7 carries a standing note: *"Note: Purchasing agent to confirm with owner the quantity of table and chairs in the meeting space."* — it applies to meeting-room furniture, not these lamps, but shows quantities on this set are not fully final.
5. PA-202 (3 tags, at Womens 019 / Mens 020 / Unisex 027 restrooms, paired with PA-502) was observed but is outside this takeoff's scope.
