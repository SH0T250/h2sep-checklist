## CRITICAL STRUCTURAL FINDING — there is NO quantity column

The printed columns are: **TYPE | DESCRIPTION | MANUFACTURER AND MODEL | MOUNTING | LAMP (NO. / TYPE) | FIXTURE WATTS | NOTES**.
The "LAMP NO." column is the number of lamps **per fixture** (e.g., SB = 2 lights per pole), **not** a building fixture count. **Building/order quantities are ABSENT from E101 and are UNVERIFIED from this sheet** — they must be counted from the floor plans (E200–E204 / lighting plans). Do not use the LAMP NO. column as an order quantity.

2. **Electrical Specifications item 17:** "PROVIDE SPECIFIED LIGHT FIXTURES WITH FINAL LOCATIONS PER THE ARCHITECT."
3. **Schedule header note:** "(ALL LIGHTING FIXTURES WILL BE VERIFIED BY ARCHITECT/OWNER PRIOR TO INSTALLATION.)"
4. **General Notes (bottom-left "NOTES" block) item 1:** "WHERE A LUMINAIRE IS INSTALLED OVER A BATHTUB OR IN A SHOWER STALL, IT SHALL BE OF THE ENCLOSED AND GASKETED TYPE LISTED FOR WET LOCATIONS. NEC 550.14(D)." Item 2: "FOR EMERGENCY POWER (90 MINUTES MINIMUM)".
5. **Lamps included/excluded with fixtures:** The schedule itself makes **no statement** that lamps are included or excluded with the fixtures. All lamp types are LED (integral-LED catalog numbers throughout). The only printed lamp exclusion on the sheet is the guarantee exclusion quoted in (1).
