## Room organisation, entry to window

The room reads as one long rectangle entered from a corridor at the short end.

1. ENTRY / KITCHENETTE ZONE, hard floor. Large format grey porcelain tile laid in a running bond.
   Guest entry door is a flat slab in a medium grey-brown woodgrain, grey painted hollow metal
   frame, lever handle, electronic keycard reader on the outside face, peephole. Corridor side
   wallpaper is a busy multicoloured geometric on white (red, gold, navy, grey ticks).
2. Immediately inside on one wall: the KITCHENETTE run. Counter is white solid surface with a
   stainless undermount sink and a chrome gooseneck faucet. Under counter: a stainless dishwasher
   (Danby) and base cabinets with black bar pulls, woodgrain fronts. Above: an over-the-range
   stainless microwave (Danby) mounted under an open woodgrain upper shelf unit. A full height
   stainless top-freezer refrigerator sits at the end of the run against the entry wall.
3. Opposite the kitchenette: the FULL LENGTH MIRROR, white painted frame, recessed flush into the
   wall. An electrical panel with a grey painted cover is on the same wall, and a white painted
   flush door (bathroom) sits beside it.
4. The BATHROOM opens off this entry zone.
5. WORKING WALL runs the length of the room from the kitchenette toward the window: a long
   woodgrain desk top at counter height with a task chair (mesh back, red-brown seat), a dresser
   with three drawers and black bar pulls, and a flat panel TV standing on the dresser top.
   Above the desk are TWO NAVY BLUE floating shelves, thin, cantilevered, no visible brackets.
   Beyond them a woodgrain open shelf box is fixed to the wall.
6. LOUNGE ZONE: a two seat sofa in dark grey-blue tweed, a patterned cube ottoman (navy and cream
   geometric), a floor lamp with a tapered white shade on a slim black stem, and a table lamp with
   a dark spherical base on a small woodgrain side table.
7. ART ABOVE SOFA: a single large landscape format framed print, black frame, black and white
   photograph of the grille and headlights of a vintage car.
8. A CURTAIN DIVIDER on a ceiling mounted track separates the lounge zone from the bed zone.
   Fabric is a coarse weave in warm taupe-grey, hung from large chrome grommets on an exposed rod.
   It runs from the ceiling to the floor, roughly a third of the room's width.
9. BED ZONE at the window end: a KING bed, upholstered headboard panel in dark plum-brown, mattress
   and box spring on a low dark platform base, no linens fitted in the photographs. A Beautyrest
   Hospitality label is visible on the mattress.
10. NIGHTSTANDS: light woodgrain, open shelf below, integrated power and data outlet in the top
    face. One to the left of the bed and one to the right.
11. NIGHTSTAND LIGHTING: slim chrome floor standing lamps with a white spherical globe, one at each
    side of the bed, standing on the floor beside the nightstand rather than mounted on the wall.
12. WARDROBE / LUGGAGE unit stands free against the wall near the window: light woodgrain, open
    hanging bay with a chrome rod and an LED strip above it, a grey fabric curtain on the open
    face, a bench seat below with a dark plum vinyl cushion, and a horizontal woodgrain panel above
    carrying five black wall hooks and a wall mounted reading light with a white shade.
13. WINDOW WALL: a punched window with a wide roller shade in a bold pattern, arcs and blocks in
    red, gold, navy, grey on cream. Shade is mounted in a white painted valance box. Below the
    window sits the PTAC unit, white, floor mounted, with a plain louvre face.

## Finishes

- ACCENT WALL behind the bed and continuing behind the sofa: an off white herringbone or chevron
  patterned wallcovering, tone on tone, low contrast, slightly textured.
- The wall opposite the bed and the ceiling are painted a flat warm white.
- The wall around the window reveal is painted a soft grey-blue, noticeably darker than the white.
- CARPET in the room zone: a broadloom or tile pattern of overlapping chevrons and blocks in
  charcoal, grey, mustard-gold and pale blue. Not a plain carpet.
- CEILING: flat white with a lower soffit over the entry and kitchenette zone. Ceiling devices seen:
  a white smoke detector, a white recessed round downlight in the entry, a concealed sprinkler with
  a white cover plate in the room, and a white surface mounted round fixture over the kitchenette.

## Bathroom

- Wallpaper: white ground with a fine GOLD line drawing of arcs, half rounds and hatch blocks. It
  covers all bathroom walls.
- VANITY: dark woodgrain casework on slim chrome legs, white solid surface top with an integral
  white rectangular basin, single lever chrome faucet. Below the top are two open shelf cubbies,
  no doors.
- MIRROR: a large landscape rectangle in a saturated YELLOW-ORANGE painted frame. This is the
  strongest colour accent anywhere in the room.
- Above the mirror: a horizontal linear LED vanity light, chrome or brushed metal housing.
- Two dark woodgrain shelf rails are fixed to the wall beside and below the mirror.
- TOILET: white two piece, standard height, chrome lever.
- SHOWER: alcove with a white tiled surround in a large horizontal running bond, a white one piece
  moulded shower base with a corner soap ledge and a round chrome drain, a chrome shower arm and
  head, a chrome pressure balancing trim, a chrome grab or towel bar on the glass, and a bypass
  slider glass enclosure in chrome with a vertical pull.
- Floor: the same large format grey porcelain tile as the entry.
- Ceiling: white, with a white square supply grille and a round downlight.

## What this tells the 3D work

The current viewer is an untextured massing model with flat colours and no materials. To stand
beside these photographs it needs, at minimum: the King layout above (which is NOT the QQ layout),
the patterned carpet, the herringbone accent wall, the patterned roller shade, the gold line
bathroom wallpaper, the yellow mirror frame, and warm lamp light against daylight from the window.

Numbers that are NOT in these photographs and must come from A550: room clear width and depth,
ceiling height, bathroom dimensions, and the exact position of every item along the working wall.
